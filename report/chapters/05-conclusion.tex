\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This chapter should restate the problem, summarize the final architecture, and describe the strongest empirical finding.
For SpriteCraft, the central claim will likely concern the benefit of combining stochastic diffusion candidates with deterministic structure-preserving recoloring.

The report should also be direct about limitations.
Current known gaps include RGB-only modeling despite alpha-channel preprocessing, heuristic rather than learned support ranking, and weak performance on transparent or complex tiling textures.

\section{Future Work}

Promising next steps include alpha-aware training, learned support selection, pack-specific resize policies, and evaluation tools that explicitly check tiling artifacts.
If the report is used as a thesis, this section should distinguish engineering improvements from research questions.
