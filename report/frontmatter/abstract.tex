\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Texture style transfer for pixel art is challenging, due to low resolution amplifying color errors. Diffusion models have shown promise for natural image synthesis but are predominantly trained on photographic datasets and struggle with the discrete palette structures and tileability requirements of game textures. We propose SpriteCraft, a per-pack training framework that ensembles style-conditioned diffusion with a RecolorNet for Minecraft resource pack texture generation. The system achieves good transfer results on a variety of stylized Minecraft resource packs, while being small enough to be rapidly trained on general consumer GPU hardware.
