\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

The author thanks the Minecraft modding and resource pack community for maintaining open documentation on blockstate models and texture conventions, without which the automated preprocessing pipeline would not have been feasible.

The author also expresses gratitude to the creators and rights holders of the texture sets used in this work. The preprocessing pipeline uses the Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.10 client jar as the vanilla reference pack, and the training / evaluation corpus draws on the following resource packs (versions follow the local archive names in \texttt{data/raw\_packs}):

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textit{Ashen 16x}, by Aim\_\_Boot, a 16$\times$ medieval-fantasy pack (\href{https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/ashen-16x}{CurseForge}).
    \item \textit{Bare Bones 1.21.11}, by robotpants, a minimalist pack designed to reproduce the visual style of Minecraft promotional art (\href{https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/bb}{CurseForge}).
    \item \textit{Cartoon Texture Pack}, port of the 4J Studios texture pack by BrandonItaly, Lasting Legacy (\href{https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/cartoon-texture-pack}{Modrinth}).
    \item \textit{Chibli 64x Freepack}, by batusaimc, an anime/cartoon-themed 64$\times$ pack (\href{https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/chibli}{CurseForge}).
    \item \textit{Clarity}, by SCtester, a 32$\times$ vanilla-adjacent pack (\href{https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/clarity}{CurseForge}).
    \item \textit{Faithful 32x}, by the Faithful Resource Pack team (\href{https://faithfulpack.net/}{faithfulpack.net}).
    \item \textit{Lithos}, credited in-pack to Eleazzaar and friends (\href{https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/lithos-core-32x-1-6-1-11-complete}{CurseForge}).
